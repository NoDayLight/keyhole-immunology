# Frozen data provenance

Runtime assets and their complete provenance, transformations, licenses/terms, citations, counts, and SHA-256 values are shipped inside the package at [`src/keyhole/resources/data/SOURCES.md`](../src/keyhole/resources/data/SOURCES.md).

The top-level `data/examples/` directory intentionally retains the real 100-row SKCM and PAAD demonstration MAFs plus their complete frozen cBioPortal source archives. No example row is invented.
