# R8 presentation-grade-report requirements

## Intent
Redesign the generated offline report as an inspectable scientific instrument that reads in a three-minute presentation and still withstands inspection. Rendering, typography, and interaction may change; no scientific value, threshold, seed, ordering, or truth boundary may. The binder is never retrained.

## Requirements
1. The report shall establish one restrained visual system with a single interactive accent, three non-overlapping type roles (serif prose, monospace data, sans controls), and hairline surfaces. Decorative per-section index labels shall be removed; the narrative index shall exist exactly once, in a persistent rail with an active-section state.
2. Every scientific visualization shall use one enforced figure anatomy carrying a persistent, precise truth label that cannot scroll out of view or be styled away, plus a legend for every colour channel actually drawn and a disclosure containing the exact serialized values behind the picture.
3. Molecular scenes shall use a real WebGL renderer built on an official, exact-pinned three.js distribution packaged locally under package resources and inlined byte-exact, with its license and SHA-256 recorded. No CDN, runtime import, remote texture, external font, network request, or `eval` is permitted, and no repository data may leave the document.
4. The renderer shall keep a maintainable scene graph, camera, lighting, resize, lifecycle, and disposal design; prefer instanced or shared geometry; provide adaptive quality for weaker GPUs and a truthful non-WebGL fallback; and support pointer, touch, keyboard, reset, and reduced-motion behaviour.
5. Experimental scenes shall draw the exact packaged coordinates and reviewed chain selection, distinguish HLA, peptide, and TCR chains, use chemically meaningful colours with an explained legend, and keep deterministic atom, bond, and chain selection. No coordinate may be moved to improve a composition, and nothing may imply dynamics, docking, affinity simulation, minimisation, or electron density.
6. Candidate scenes shall preserve the exact truth boundary `Real backbone (PDB 1HHK) · mutated side chain ideal geometry — illustrative` and keep measured template coordinates visibly distinguishable from illustrative side-chain geometry. No all-atom candidate, surface, docking pose, or rotamer may be invented.
7. The funnel shall keep exactly one seeded witness per serialized candidate. Gate outcomes, colours, and counts shall derive only from serialized verdicts and reason codes; browser code shall apply no scientific threshold. A complete static and reduced-motion fallback and a selected-candidate evidence view shall remain.
8. Population coverage shall use only serialized AFR/AMR/EAS/EUR and ALL_OBSERVED values. Geography and globe geometry shall remain explicitly illustrative, ALL_OBSERVED shall never be drawn as a place or described as worldwide, SAS shall be reported absent rather than zero, and exact numeric tables shall remain available regardless of graphics support.
9. Literature evidence shall preserve train, held-out, and not-in-binding-dataset strata, nullable AUC semantics, and every denominator disclosure. Published T-cell positivity and KEYHOLE visibility shall be presented as distinct endpoints, and synthetic shuffled controls shall never be called assayed negatives, specificity, or clinical validation.
10. Desktop presentation shall be designed first, then made responsive and accessible without horizontal document overflow, preserving keyboard navigation, focus visibility, reduced-motion behaviour, readable fallbacks, teardown safety, and failure recovery.
11. No binder model, NPZ, training data, `metrics.json`, `SOURCES.md`, published metric, threshold, seed, candidate ordering, population calculation, literature entry, or schema semantic may change. Fixed-epoch SKCM `results.json` shall remain byte-identical to the approved R7 oracle.
12. Validators and tests shall not be weakened to accommodate the redesign. Assertions naming removed implementation details shall be replaced with equivalent or stronger checks, and any intentional envelope change shall be recorded with its measured cause.
